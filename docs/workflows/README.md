# Example workflows

New here? Begin with the [plain-language Start Here guide](../start-here.md). These two short examples show where an assistant might help with routine requests while keeping a person in control of live records and decisions. They are starting points for discussion, not ready-to-run instructions or business policies.

**Both examples are illustrative—not tested.** They are conceptual only: no integrations, setup, interoperability, or end-to-end workflow have been verified. See the [evidence-label guide](../evidence-labels.md) for what this label means.

## Choose an example

- [New service-request intake](service-request-intake.md) — capture a request for a person to review.
- [Appointment scheduling or change](appointment-scheduling.md) — prepare a proposed booking or change for explicit approval.

## Developer handoff

For either example, first identify the actual live system of record (the authoritative place where the business keeps that information) and its owner. Define permissions, consent, approval, exceptions, and read-back checks for the real environment before implementation. These examples do not establish an integration, setup, or authority to act. No message may be sent and no live record or schedule may be written without human approval and the required consent, unless a separately reviewed future workflow explicitly defines safe authority.

These examples are not professional, legal, or booking-policy advice. Adapt and review them for the relevant business and jurisdiction.
